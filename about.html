The Situation

38 Years of Trust. Zero Web Presence.

Triple C's Plumbing had been serving the Mt. Clemens area for nearly four decades. Their reputation was earned one job at a time. But when potential customers searched for them online — nothing came up.

No website. No Google presence. No way for happy customers to leave a review that the next customer could find.

We built them a clean, professional website and helped them start turning satisfied customers into visible, searchable five-star reviews.

The Founder

Why I Built This

Virtual Labor Force started with a simple observation: thousands of local trade businesses across Oakland, Macomb, and Wayne County have built real reputations over decades — but they're invisible online.

I found Triple C's Plumbing on Google Maps while prospecting for businesses with no website. Thirty-eight years in business. Zero web presence. I reached out, built them a site, and they were live within days.

That's the model. No jargon. No enterprise price tags. No months-long projects. Just fast, clean, affordable work that gets local businesses found — and keeps them growing.

Virtual Labor Force is built on open-source tools, practical systems, and a belief that the best technology should be accessible to any business, regardless of size.
